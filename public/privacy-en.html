Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 22, 2026

At "Coro LLDM" (hereinafter, "the Application") we deeply value your privacy and autonomy. Our application has been designed with a primary focus on offline functionality and minimizing data collection. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you use our platform.

1. Personal Data Collection

We do not require mandatory registration. The Application can be fully used without the need to create a user account, guaranteeing total anonymity during your rehearsals and performances. We do not collect, request, or store sensitive personal data such as names, physical addresses, phone numbers, or invasive access to your contacts or camera.

2. Usage and Operational Data

All information generated by your direct use (such as your sheet music preferences, playlists, or player settings) is processed and stored strictly locally on your device. We only require a sporadic internet connection to:

  • Download and cache the sheet music catalog (PDF) and audio tracks (MIDI) required for rehearsal.
  • Validate premium subscription licenses to prevent unauthorized use.

3. Advertisements and Advertising Providers

To keep the application free and accessible to all choirs, we use advertising services provided by third parties (such as Google's networks). These advertising partners may use standardized technologies and anonymous device identifiers (such as your phone's Advertising ID) to show relevant ads. You can disable or reset this identifier at any time from the privacy settings of your Android or iOS device.

4. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

If you decide to purchase our Premium version ("PRO") to remove ads, all transactions are processed securely through official stores (Google Play Store or Apple App Store) and our certified revenue managers. We never have access to your credit card information, bank accounts, or financial history.

5. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures (encrypted data transmission) during catalog synchronizations to protect the integrity of the sheet music and ensure you receive the correct files from our servers.

6. Changes to this Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be reflected on this same page with a new update date.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about our privacy practices or the offline operation of the application, you can contact the support team at: huritolentino@gmail.com
